## Important attribution caveat

The first volume's translation is **P. E. Pusey's** (Edward Bouverie Pusey's son), not E. B. Pusey's. The preface is mixed-authorship: the 1885 second-volume preface explicitly states that **the greater part of the 1874 first-volume preface was written by Dr. E. B. Pusey**, principally on the Procession of the Holy Spirit. Do not index the whole preface under E. B. Pusey's name until its precise E. B. / P. E. portions can be distinguished. Do not index Cyril's commentary or the son's translation.

Independent source for this attribution: [1885 preface, volume II, pages iii–xvi](https://www.tertullian.org/fathers/cyril_on_john_00_intro2.htm).
